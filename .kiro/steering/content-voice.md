# Copy voice

- First person where it is Perdi talking, otherwise plain statements. Concrete over abstract: "Planner that sizes solar and diesel for island grids" beats "innovative energy solution".
- Headings are short and a little unexpected, sections have a one-word name (Craft, Journey, Foundations, Recognition, Connect). Keep that pattern.
- No marketing filler ("passionate", "cutting-edge", "seamless", "leverage"), no exclamation marks, no emoji.
- One italic accent word per heading at most.
- Alt text describes what the screenshot shows ("Dashboard listing boat positions on a map"), never "image of".
- Button labels are verbs: "Copy email", "View project".
- Dates as "Mar 2024". Ranges with an en dash and spaces: "Mar 2024 – Present".
