# Product

Personal portfolio and developer blog of Perdi (software engineer, Makassar, Indonesia). Goal of the redesign: one coherent, editorial design applied to every page, with the home page `/` as the source of truth.

- Audience: hiring managers and engineers who skim fast, and developers who read the blog.
- The site must feel hand-made: specific copy, real screenshots of real projects, no stock phrases.
- Content lives in `src/content` (collections `blog`, `projects`) and `src/config/` (author, socials, experience, achievements). Do not hardcode that data in components.
- Pages in scope for the rewrite: home, about, projects list and detail, blog list, post, archive, tags, categories, skills, search, 404.
